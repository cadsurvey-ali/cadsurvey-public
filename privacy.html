Privacy Policy

CAD & SURVEY Tools for AutoCAD and Civil 3D · By Eng. Ali

Effective date: 8 November 2026

In short: CAD & SURVEY Tools work on your computer. They do not collect analytics, do not track you, and never upload your drawings or project data. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us yourself by e-mail or WhatsApp.

1. Who we are

CAD & SURVEY Tools ("the tools") is a plug-in for Autodesk AutoCAD and Civil 3D developed by Eng. Ali Abdulkhaleq Dakhil Hilfi, Basra, Iraq. Contact: eng.alicadsurvey@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your computer

  • Your drawings, coordinates, reports and all project data. The tools never send them anywhere.
  • Tool settings, the activity log, and the information you enter in My Profile.
  • Your licence / activation status.

3. The only automatic internet connection

When AutoCAD starts, and when you press Check for updates, the tools download a small public text file (links and the latest version number) from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), or from its mirror on jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) if GitHub is not reachable.

  • This is a download only. The request contains just the tool name and version number.
  • No personal data, computer ID, licence, e-mail or drawing content is sent.
  • The update check is a local comparison of version numbers on your computer.
  • Like any website, GitHub and jsDelivr can see your computer's IP address under their own privacy policies.

4. Activation

Activation works without an internet connection:

  • The tools create a short Computer ID from Windows' internal machine identifier. It is used only to link your activation code to your computer.
  • The tools prepare an activation request (with the Computer ID) and open your e-mail program (or Gmail in your browser, or WhatsApp, as you choose). You decide whether to send it. Nothing is sent automatically.
  • The activation code is checked on your computer with a digital signature, without contacting any server.

5. Support package (optional)

If you use Help & support, the tools create a ZIP file on your desktop that you may send to us yourself. It can contain: a screenshot of the AutoCAD window, your message, the last 100 warning / error lines of the tools log (optional, no drawing content), version and system information (tools, AutoCAD, .NET, Windows, screen and language), licence status with the Computer ID, and any file you choose to add. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

6. Links you open yourself

Some buttons open external services only when you click them: our YouTube channel, Instagram, Facebook group, e-mail (your mail program or Gmail in your browser) and WhatsApp. The map button in some tools opens Google Maps at the selected point, which sends that point's coordinates to Google. KML files exported by the tools, when opened in Google Earth, load standard point icons from Google. These services are covered by their own privacy policies.

7. Information we receive from you

When you request a trial, buy a subscription, or ask for support, we receive only what you send us: for example your name, e-mail address, the activation request / Computer ID, a payment receipt image, and any support package or message. We use it only to activate your licence, send you your code and receipt, and help you. We do not sell or share it with third parties, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

8. What the tools do not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or tracking.
  • No upload of drawings, files or project data.
  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. For any privacy question or a deletion request, write to eng.alicadsurvey@gmail.com.
